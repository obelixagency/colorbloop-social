Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Colorbloop Automation is an internal tool used only by Colorbloop to publish its own content to its own Facebook page, Instagram account and YouTube channel.

It uses Google/YouTube and Meta permissions solely to upload videos and posts to accounts owned by Colorbloop. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users. Authorization tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are used only to publish Colorbloop content.

You can revoke access at any time from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Facebook settings. Contact: via our Facebook page messages.
